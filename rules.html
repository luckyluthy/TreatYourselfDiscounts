Pond Rules

Welcome to the Pond! A few house rules keep the deals flowing and the flock happy.

1. Be kind, be respectful

Everyone here is hunting deals together. Treat fellow ducklings the way you want to be treated.

2. Share safe links only

No suspicious links, no malware, no shady shorteners. If a link looks sketchy, it does not belong in the Pond.

3. Post in the right channel

Keep things organized so everyone can find what they came for. Deals go where they belong.

4. Deals come with disclosure

Some links on this page and in the Pond are affiliate links. If you shop through them, the Pond may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.
