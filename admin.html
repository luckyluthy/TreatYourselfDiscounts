Your member list in one place: everyone with a Pond account, when they joined, and the recipes they have saved. Sign in here with your own Pond account. Only the owner account can open this list, anyone else gets turned away by the database itself.

No Pond account of your own yet? Make one first on the Pond Account page, then tell Goose, who flips the owner switch for your account.

Send a deal alert 🔔

Write a deal once, send it two ways. Post alert puts it in every member's Pond Account under Deal Alerts, so they see it next time they visit your site. Email it too opens your own mail app with every member hidden in BCC and the alert written out, you just tap send.

Posting needs you signed in above with your owner account. Real phone push notifications (the kind that buzz a phone with the site closed) are a separate add-on, ask Goose about OneSignal if you want those. Text messages need a paid texting service and members' phone numbers, which the site does not collect yet.
