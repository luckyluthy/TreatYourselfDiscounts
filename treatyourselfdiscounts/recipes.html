Churned Vanilla Ice Cream

Lucy's homemade churned ice cream, rich and simple, the way a small batch should be.

You need

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch of salt

How to make it

  1. Whisk the milk, sugar, and salt until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Stir in the cream and vanilla, then chill the mix until cold, at least 2 hours or overnight.
  3. Pour into your ice cream maker and churn until thick and soft serve smooth, about 20 to 25 minutes.
  4. Scoop into a container and freeze about 4 hours for firm scoops.

Fermented Root Beer

Old fashioned root beer with real fizz from a short ferment. Use plastic bottles so pressure stays safe and easy to check.

You need

  • 1 gallon water, plus a little extra
  • 1/4 cup sassafras root bark (or a root beer extract, if you prefer)
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1/8 teaspoon champagne yeast (or ale yeast)
  • Clean plastic soda bottles with caps

How to make it

  1. Simmer the sassafras in the water for about 20 minutes, take it off the heat, and stir in the sugar until dissolved. Let it cool to lukewarm.
  2. Sprinkle the yeast over a cup of the warm liquid, rest 10 minutes, then stir it back into the pot.
  3. Funnel into plastic bottles, leaving an inch or two of head space, and cap tightly.
  4. Rest at room temperature 2 to 3 days, squeezing a bottle each day; when it feels firm with fizz, move the bottles to the fridge right away. The cold stops the ferment.
  5. Drink within a few weeks, and open slowly over the sink, homemade fizz is enthusiastic.
Weeknight favorite

Chicken Wraps

Lucy's chicken wraps, the easy Pond dinner: warm tortillas, juicy chicken, crunch, and a sauce you like.

You need

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder, 1 teaspoon paprika, salt and pepper
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • Shredded lettuce, sliced tomato, shredded cheese
  • Ranch or your favorite sauce

How to make it

  1. Season the chicken and cook in a hot pan until golden and cooked through, about 6 to 7 minutes per side. Rest 5 minutes, then slice.
  2. Warm the tortillas so they bend without cracking.
  3. Layer lettuce, tomato, chicken, and cheese down the middle, drizzle with sauce.
  4. Fold the sides in, roll up tight, and toast seam side down in the pan for a minute if you like it crispy.
